1. Ubuntu arm-linux-gnueabihf-gcc 13 — wrong -march

  GCC 13 rejects -march=armv7-a without -mfpu=vfp during the
  kernel's cc-option probe, so the build silently fell back to
  -march=armv5t.

  Fix: export KCFLAGS="-march=armv7-a -mfpu=vfp" in the script.

2. Binutils 2.42 — #alloc section syntax

  Kernel 5.4.31 uses Solaris-style .section ..., #alloc, which
  binutils 2.41+ no longer accepts on ARM.

  Fix: Updated arch/arm/**/*.S to GNU style ("a", "ax") — upstream
  patch 790756c7e022 (https://git.kernel.org/pub/scm/linux/kernel/g
  it/stable/linux.git/commit/?id=790756c7e022).

3. GCC 13 — array_index_nospec in libahci.c

  EM_MAX_SLOTS is in a wide enum; GCC 13 makes sizeof(enum) > 
  sizeof(long) on ARM, tripping BUILD_BUG_ON.

  Fix: Cast to (int)EM_MAX_SLOTS in drivers/ata/libahci.c.
